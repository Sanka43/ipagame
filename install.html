How to Install IPA

Play IPA games on your iPhone with LiveContainer.

An iPhone only runs apps that are signed for it. LiveContainer is one app you sign once; every game you add runs inside it. You can add as many games as you like, and only LiveContainer needs refreshing every 7 days.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad
  • A Windows PC or Mac and a USB cable (only for the first setup)
  • A free Apple ID
  • The game's IPA file from this store

Step 1 · Install SideStore

  1. Follow the setup on to install SideStore with your computer and cable, and sign in with your Apple ID.
  2. On the iPhone open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and tap Trust for your Apple ID.
  3. On iOS 16 or later turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode and restart the iPhone.

Step 2 · Install LiveContainer

  1. In Safari download the LiveContainer IPA from its official releases page:
  2. Open SideStore → My Apps, tap + and pick the LiveContainer IPA from Files.

Step 3 · Add a game

  1. Open the game in this store and tap Download to get its IPA. Safari saves it to the Files app (Downloads).
  2. Open LiveContainer and tap + at the top.
  3. Pick the IPA you downloaded. LiveContainer imports it and adds it to the list.
  4. Tap the game and tap Run. That's it, enjoy!

To start a game straight from the Home Screen, open its settings in LiveContainer and use Add to Home Screen.

Keep it working

With a free Apple ID, SideStore and LiveContainer stop opening after 7 days. Open SideStore → My Apps and tap Refresh All before then. Your games and saves stay.

Problems?

  • “Untrusted Developer” — trust your Apple ID in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  • The game closes as soon as it opens — some games need JIT. Turn on JIT for the game in LiveContainer's settings (it uses SideStore), then run it again.
  • The import fails — download the IPA again; the file may not have finished downloading.
  • LiveContainer won't open — it has passed 7 days; refresh it in SideStore.
