Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

This policy explains what Game Store (app.ipagame.store) keeps about you when you create an account, and what it is used for. We only keep what the store needs to work.

What we keep

  • Account details — your username and email address.
  • Password — stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt). We can't see or recover it.
  • Profile photo — a photo you upload, or the photo on your Google account if you sign in with Google. Without one we show your Gravatar, which is looked up from a hash of your email.
  • Google sign-in — if you use “Continue with Google”, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else.
  • Account activity — when you joined, when you last signed in, and whether your email is verified.
  • Security records — to stop password guessing and spam we note sign-in attempts and code emails per IP address. These records are deleted automatically after one day.
  • Cookie — a single session cookie keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To email you sign-up verification and password-reset codes from info@ipagame.store. We don't send marketing email.
  • To show your username and photo in the store.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it except with the services needed to run the store: our web host, which stores the data and sends our emails, Google (only if you choose Google sign-in) and Gravatar (for the default photo).

Your choices

  • Change or remove your profile photo at any time on the Account page.
  • Delete your account at any time from the Account page (“Delete Account”). Your account details and uploaded photo are removed straight away.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Game Store's access in your Google account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: info@ipagame.store.
